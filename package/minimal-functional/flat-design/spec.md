# Flat Design

## Signature
solid color blocks, two-dimensional icons, simple geometry, no gradients,
little or no shadow
high legibility

## Suited for
mobile apps, dashboards, utilities, cross-platform products

## Layout
divide the interface into crisp two-dimensional zones, align modules to a
simple grid
show hierarchy through size and placement instead of overlap or elevation

## Colors
- primary: strongest flat brand hue
- secondary: clearly separated complementary hue
- base-100: solid white
- base-200: pale neutral
- base-300: light gray

## Imagery
flat vector illustrations, pictograms,
and icons built from a shared geometric vocabulary with minimal internal shading

## Density
medium density with compact scan groups; separate related tools through color
blocks and spacing
not nested surface effects

## Motion
planar translation, scaling, and color changes with direct timing
elements move like graphic layers rather than physical objects

## Shape
circles, rectangles, simple silhouettes with a consistent radius system,
crisp edges
no simulated bevels
`--radius-*` between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
sturdy sans-serif with clear bold weights, concise labels
avoid hairline text that loses definition against solid color
Suggested: Poppins, Montserrat, Rubik, Lato, Open Sans

## Origins
late 2000s–mid-2010s; mobile and operating-system interface design
shift away from richly simulated materials toward simple shapes, solid color,
and typography
— Microsoft Metro on Windows Phone (~2010) a major early expression
Apple's iOS 7 redesign (2013) made it mainstream
