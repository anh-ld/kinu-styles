# Millennial Beige

## Signature
layered warm neutrals, intentional empty space, serif display type, quiet
sans-serif copy
faint natural texture
soft daylight, restrained organic forms; `--noise: 1`

## Suited for
wellness, skincare, home and lifestyle commerce, hospitality, portfolios,
calm productivity tools
refined consumer services

## Layout
restrained grid, broad margins, few generously spaced content groups
one product/message/action anchoring each section

## Colors
- primary: muted clay or caramel
- secondary: dusty taupe
- base-100: warm cream
- base-200: pale sand
- base-300: greige
- accents: caramel or muted clay, reserved
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
softly lit editorial photography: linen, pale wood, ceramics, stone, handmade
objects
warm skin tones
preserve grain and material variation so neutrals feel tactile

## Density
low to moderate, deliberate breathing room, concise copy, clearly grouped
forms or products
avoid crowded grids and empty space that hides the next action

## Motion
unhurried fades, gentle vertical reveals, soft image dissolves, subtle tonal
hovers; no bounce
parallax, gloss
or looping spectacle

## Shape
broad rectangles, modest rounded corners, thin warm-taupe borders, shallow
tonal elevation
occasional arches or softly irregular organic silhouettes; `--radius-*`
0.25rem–1rem

## Typography
serif for titles and display, restrained sans for body, labels, navigation,
controls, and data
relaxed line spacing, clear weight hierarchy
Suggested: Cormorant Garamond, Lora, DM Serif Display; Inter, Manrope

## Origins
late 2010s–2020s; global lifestyle branding, social media, interiors
informal retrospective label overlapping "Austurbane" beige/stone/fabric staging
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/austurbane
