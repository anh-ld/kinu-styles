# Cyberminimalism

## Signature
black-and-white fields, oversized grotesque typography, strict bento grids,
simple circles
and squares
isolated product renders, restrained gradients

## Suited for
technology brands, premium SaaS, product launches, portfolios, finance,
architecture
concise editorial experiences

## Layout
sparse strict grid or disciplined bento system; one enormous word, isolated
object
or decisive metric as the focal point with a substantial uninterrupted field
around it

## Colors
- primary: lone functional icy-blue or spectral accent
- secondary: cool gray
- base-100/base-200/base-300: charcoal, near-black, and black (dark scheme)
- at most one spectral, grainy, or monochromatic gradient

## Imagery
isolated devices, precise object renders, macro industrial details, clean
diagrams
abstract smooth geometry
tightly art-directed product crops on empty grounds

## Density
hero regions extremely low, bento regions moderate; concise labels
detailed product or data content progressively revealed

## Motion
slow exact wipes, modular grid resizing, controlled gradient travel, smooth
object rotation
restrained type reveals; no ornamental looping clutter

## Shape
perfect squares, circles, razor-thin rules, flush rectangles
a few carefully targeted glass or softly modeled surfaces rather than a full
card vocabulary
`--radius-*` set to 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
one contemporary grotesque across extreme display scales and very small labels
tracking and line breaks controlled precisely, no decorative type mixing
Suggested: Space Grotesk, Inter, Sora, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Sans

## Origins
retrospective label for late-1990s–2000s sources; current revival; technology
fashion
science-fiction imagery
product graphics, online taxonomy; no founding manifesto or stable historical
definition
— classifies the restrained side of turn-of-the-millennium cyber design
the word also has an unrelated meaning in digital-wellbeing writing
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics, cari.institute/aesthetics/y2k-aesthetic
