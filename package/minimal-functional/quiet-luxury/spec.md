# Quiet Luxury

## Signature
generous whitespace, muted neutrals, refined serif type, subtle hairlines,
restrained motion
premium photography

## Suited for
luxury commerce, hospitality, architecture, finance, beauty, high-end
professional services

## Layout
expansive margins, measured editorial alignment, a small number of impeccably
spaced modules
product or service detail unfolds slowly

## Colors
- primary: deep brown or navy
- secondary: muted brass or low-chroma brand tone
- base-100: warm ivory
- base-200: pale stone
- base-300: taupe

## Imagery
high-resolution material studies, architectural space, restrained portraiture
product photography with soft directional light and precise crops

## Density
low density, concise confident copy
secondary specifications hidden behind elegant progressive disclosure

## Motion
slow short-distance fades, image dissolves, precise hover responses; no
bounce, spectacle
or continuous ambient movement

## Shape
refined rectangles, hairline borders, subtle insets, small or absent radii;
material
and spacing communicate value
`--radius-*` set to 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
refined serif or neo-grotesque display paired with immaculate body
typography; careful tracking
restrained weights, generous line spacing
Suggested: Cormorant Garamond, Bodoni Moda, Instrument Serif, DM Serif
Display, Manrope

## Origins
longstanding discreet-luxury lineage; trend label surged in 2023; European
and American fashion discourse
— understated expensive materials and unbranded tailoring far older than the
phrase
became a dominant fashion and lifestyle label in 2023, amplified by runway
coverage and television
then migrated into branding and UI; a media trend category, not a school with
a single founder
