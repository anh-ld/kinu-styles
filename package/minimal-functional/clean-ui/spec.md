# Clean UI

## Signature
tight spacing, measured grids, layered surfaces, hairline borders, subtle
shadows, inset controls
restrained radii, precise micro-details

## Suited for
dashboards, admin panels, enterprise software, developer tools, settings,
data products
component-heavy web apps

## Layout
strict 12-column grid, predictable navigation, aligned page headers,
repeatable sections
controlled gaps, insets
and baselines; cards only when they clarify grouping

## Colors
- primary: subdued brand blue
- secondary: muted slate or teal
- base-100: white
- base-200: off-white
- base-300: light gray
- depth: `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
kept subordinate to the information grid; small avatars, product thumbnails,
precise diagrams
restrained data visualization over decorative illustration or cinematic
photography

## Density
medium to high via compact controls, tables, filters, forms
tight local grouping with airy rhythm between sections; use `*-sm` size variants

## Motion
measured fades, short slides, focus rings, small state transitions for menus,
dialogs, sorting
validation
no ambient or theatrical motion

## Shape
restrained rounded geometry, plain rectangles, compact pills, inset controls
soft depth from hairline borders
shallow shadows, layered surfaces; `--radius-*` between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
quiet neutral sans-serif with strong hierarchy, compact scale, medium-weight
labels
muted supporting text
tabular numerals; hierarchy by size, weight, alignment, not expressive styling
Suggested: Inter, Geist, Instrument Sans, DM Sans, IBM Plex Sans

## Origins
2010s–present; digital product teams and platform design systems
inherits modernist information design
repeatable components grew with large platform systems such as Material
Design (Google
2014) and the product-design systems that followed
