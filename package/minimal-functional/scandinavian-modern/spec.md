# Scandinavian Modern

## Signature
light neutrals, clean lines, warm wood tones, functional spacing, humanist type
subtle color accents

## Suited for
furniture, lifestyle, wellness, architecture, commerce, productivity products

## Layout
airy functional modules, calm asymmetry, generous margins
clean product grids balanced with warm lifestyle or interior scenes

## Colors
- primary: muted blue or forest green
- secondary: clay
- base-100: white
- base-200: oatmeal
- base-300: pale wood-gray

## Imagery
daylight interiors, honest material details, useful objects, landscapes
relaxed human moments with minimal staging

## Density
low to medium, favoring practical clarity, concise descriptions,
and open space that conveys ease and order

## Motion
subtle fades, short slides, quiet hover feedback with natural timing
no decorative loops or dramatic depth effects

## Shape
clean lines, simple rectangles, modest soft corners, fine dividers,
occasional warm material blocks
`--radius-*` between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
friendly humanist sans-serif with optional restrained serif accent;
comfortable sizes, open counters
uncomplicated hierarchy
Suggested: Karla, Figtree, Manrope, Source Sans 3, Lora

## Origins
1920s roots, international prominence in the 1940s–1960s; Denmark, Sweden,
Finland, Norway, Iceland
developed through Nordic architecture, furniture, glass, textile, and product
design
— functional production joined with human scale, craft knowledge, light, and
natural materials
"Danish Modern" a particularly influential export
Sources: designmuseum.dk/en/exhibition/danish-modern/
