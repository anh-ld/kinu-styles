# Lo-fi Art

## Signature
cozy anime scenes, rainy windows, headphones and books, warm lamp light,
muted night palettes
film grain and VHS noise; `--noise: 1;`

## Suited for
music, focus and study tools, journaling, wellness, streaming, quietly
atmospheric communities

## Layout
one room, train, cafe, or window view as the immersive main canvas
navigation and playback controls in a quiet edge overlay that does not
fragment the scene

## Colors
- primary: warm lamp amber or peach
- secondary: dusty indigo or soft teal
- base-100: muted night blue
- base-200: deep mauve-blue
- base-300: dark indigo
- texture: `--noise: 1;`

## Imagery
solitary reading, studying, drawing, or listening in familiar interiors with
pets, plants, books
headphones, rain
and a lived-in night city beyond

## Density
rich environmental detail in the illustration; low interface density, brief copy
visually subdued task controls

## Motion
seamless low-amplitude loops: falling rain, drifting steam, turning pages,
blinking screens
breathing figures
occasional pet movement

## Shape
ordinary window frames, desks, shelves, soft furniture silhouettes, modest
rectangular overlays
no futuristic chrome or decorative card systems; `--radius-*` between 0.25rem
and 1rem

## Typography
unobtrusive friendly sans-serif or soft book face; modest hierarchy; ambient
illustration
not display lettering
sets the mood
Suggested: M PLUS Rounded 1c, Nunito Sans, Quicksand, Zen Kaku Gothic New, Lora

## Origins
late 20th-century music and print precedents; digital label in the 2010s
Origin: independent recording, photocopied print culture, online illustration
"Lo-fi" first described production whose audible limitations remained present
then became a broader label for intentionally modest or imperfect media
visual overlaps with photocopied zines
grunge typography, rough scans, and casual digital drawing; a contemporary
cross-media category
not a historically chartered school
Sources: thegraphicdesignschool.com, vam.ac.uk
