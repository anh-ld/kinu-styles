# Whimsigothic

## Signature
moons and stars, jewel tones, mystical symbols, ornate curves, playful gothic
type
celestial illustration

## Suited for
beauty, music, astrology, fashion, entertainment, imaginative lifestyle brands

## Layout
theatrical celestial scenes around radial charts, framed stories, and layered
nighttime panels
balance mysticism with a clear central action

## Colors
- primary: plum or emerald
- secondary: antique gold or ruby
- base-100: midnight blue
- base-200: inky violet
- base-300: black
- midnight blue, plum, emerald, ruby, moonlit cream,
  and antique gold in jewel-like combinations with deep shadow

## Imagery
moons, stars, tarot-like figures, velvet, candles, celestial maps, ornate
botanicals
playful mystical characters

## Density
medium visual and content density; symbolic details clustered around
headings; readings, products
or controls stay legible

## Motion
orbit small celestial symbols, twinkle selected stars, open card-like panels
reveal constellations with slow theatrical timing

## Shape
crescent moons, stars, arches, ornate curves, medallions, pointed frames
curling botanical motifs with a playful gothic edge; `--radius-*` between
0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
whimsical Gothic or uncial display face paired with a refined serif body
ornate capitals limited to short magical statements
Suggested: Cinzel Decorative, Grenze Gotisch, Cormorant Unicase, Marcellus
SC, Uncial Antiqua

## Origins
1990s source period; label coined in 2020
Origin: a retrospective internet classification of 1990s fashion, interiors,
and media
A new name for an older cluster of celestial, bohemian, gothic
and Art Nouveau-inflected imagery visible in 1990s popular culture
researcher Evan Collins coined the term in 2020; because the category was
assembled retrospectively
the designers and audiences of the original period did not generally call
their work Whimsigothic
