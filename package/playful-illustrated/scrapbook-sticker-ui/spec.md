# Scrapbook / Sticker UI

## Signature
sticker clusters, masking tape, snapshots, doodles, labels and stamps,
layered ephemera
`--noise: 1;` and `--depth: 1;`

## Suited for
social products, youth brands, journaling, creator tools, events, community
experiences

## Layout
journal or pinboard structure; entries chronological or clustered by theme
decorative ephemera placed around
not across, active content

## Colors
- primary: recurring ink or tape hue
- secondary: one representative sticker color
- base-100: warm paper
- base-200: notebook cream
- base-300: light cork
- texture: `--noise: 1;`
- depth: `--depth: 1;`

## Imagery
snapshot crops, instant-film frames, doodles, ticket stubs, stamps, tape
user-generated media with believable scale and layering

## Density
medium-to-high personal detail; entries separated by page margins
each handwritten callout kept short

## Motion
peel stickers, press stamps, slide snapshots under tape
draw doodles in brief tactile sequences triggered by user actions

## Shape
die-cut sticker outlines, scallops, torn notes, tape strips, label makers
imperfect snapshot rectangles
`--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
handwriting reserved for annotations and names; longer content in a readable
sans-serif or serif
vary rotation without harming scan order
Suggested: Schoolbell, Patrick Hand, Special Elite, Caveat, Gochi Hand

## Origins
19th-century domestic precedents; digital revival in the 2010s–2020s
Origin: personal albums, fan culture, stationery, social media
Scrapbooks long predate digital interfaces — 19th-century albums combined
clippings, cards, drawings
and personal annotation as assembled records; Sticker UI turns that additive
authored surface into a screen language shaped by digital journaling,
messaging stickers, fandom
and social platforms; a contemporary synthesis, not a single historical school
