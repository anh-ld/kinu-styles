# Corporate Memphis

## Signature
flat vector people, exaggerated proportions, geometric environments, bright
solid colors
simplified faces
friendly scenes

## Suited for
SaaS onboarding, explainers, fintech, HR products, service brands, friendly
corporate communication

## Layout
concise product content paired with one large illustrative scene
smaller modular vignettes explain steps
benefits, or empty states

## Colors
- primary: brightest brand hue
- secondary: softer contrasting fill
- base-100: pale cream
- base-200: lightly tinted neutral
- base-300: soft gray
- bright brand primaries and soft secondary fills against a pale neutral
  background; skin
  and clothing colors inclusive and clearly separated

## Imagery
flat vector people with exaggerated limbs, simplified faces, geometric props
workplace or service scenarios tied directly to the message

## Density
medium to low; each scene explains one concept
no decorative characters around already complex workflows

## Motion
simple character gestures, floating props, and scene assembly with friendly
easing
the product interaction itself stays direct

## Shape
soft geometric environments, rounded rectangles, circles
abstract plant or furniture forms with consistent flat outlines; `--radius-*`
between 0.75rem
and 1.5rem

## Typography
approachable geometric sans-serif with open counters; strong sentence-case
headings
illustration more expressive than the type
Suggested: Poppins, DM Sans, Nunito Sans, Rubik, M PLUS Rounded 1c

## Origins
late 2010s–early 2020s
Origin: global technology branding; strongly associated with Facebook's 2017
Alegria system
A critical nickname for flat, friendly vector illustration used by large
technology companies
Alegria was created by Buck with illustrator Xoana Herrera for Facebook in
2017; its abstract people
bright shapes, and scalable scenes spread widely
while the "Corporate Memphis" label connected that wave
— loosely and sometimes sarcastically — to the color and geometry of 1980s
Memphis design
