# Acid Graphics

## Signature
fluorescent palette, warped typography, chrome accents, dense layering,
liquefied shapes
rave-flyer distortion
`--depth: 1;`

## Suited for
music, fashion, festivals, experimental portfolios, cultural campaigns

## Layout
layer warped headlines, event data, chrome fragments,
and liquefied imagery like an underground rave flyer
retain one fixed panel for practical details

## Colors
- fluorescent yellow, green, orange, pink, and blue against black or metallic gray
  deliberately abrasive high-contrast combinations
- primary: fluorescent yellow or green
- secondary: hot pink or electric blue
- base-100: metallic gray
- base-200: charcoal
- base-300: black

## Imagery
distorted portraits, liquefied objects, chrome type, thermal color, scanned
flyers
stretched textures, club photography

## Density
high visual and textual density; isolate time, place, price,
and primary action from the most distorted layers

## Motion
warp type, pulse fluorescent fields, liquefy transitions
brief strobe-like cuts only within safe frequency and reduced-motion limits

## Shape
liquid spikes, stretched blobs, chrome thorns, distorted grids, concentric
targets
irregular flyer crops
`--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
warped experimental lettering for dominant names; condensed sans-serif with
firm alignment
and uncompromised readability for logistics
Suggested: Rubik Glitch, Climate Crisis, Unbounded, Syne, Archivo Black

## Origins
mid-2010s–present; online graphic design, club culture, independent fashion,
and social media
catalogued by CARI as Acidgrafix; combines chrome type, distressed textures,
3D experiments
horror vacui
and self-referential software imagery; extends rave, grunge,
and early web references through contemporary tools and image-sharing platforms
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/acidgrafix
