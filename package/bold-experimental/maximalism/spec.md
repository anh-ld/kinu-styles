# Maximalism

## Signature
dense layering, clashing patterns, ornate typography, vivid palette, mixed
imagery
abundant decoration

## Suited for
fashion, entertainment, cultural brands, portfolios, campaign websites

## Layout
layer pattern, image, type, and ornament around a deliberate focal hierarchy
repeat key anchors so users can navigate the abundance confidently

## Colors
- broad palette unified by one dominant ground color and recurring accent
  relationships
  test every dense overlap for readable contrast
- primary: most repeated accent
- secondary: its recurring counterpoint
- base-100: restrained tonal step of dominant ground color
- base-200: restrained tonal step of dominant ground color
- base-300: restrained tonal step of dominant ground color

## Imagery
art-directed photography, illustration, texture, pattern
decorative objects combined through a consistent cultural or narrative concept

## Density
high visual and editorial density; small pockets of plain space around
actions, prices, labels
and long-form reading

## Motion
animate selected layers in coordinated groups; theatrical reveals
and depth changes while the rest of the composition stays still

## Shape
ornate frames, patterned fields, layered cutouts, flourishes
varied silhouettes linked by repeated motifs rather than one radius system
`--radius-*` between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
two or three expressive families with a disciplined body face; repeat
hierarchy consistently
decorative type never carries essential instructions
Suggested: Bodoni Moda, Syne, Unbounded, Bungee, Cormorant Garamond

## Origins
recurring across art and design; renewed digitally in the late 2010s–2020s;
multiple decorative
postmodern
and counter-minimal traditions; contemporary web maximalism draws on
postmodern graphics
collage, club culture
and the technical freedom of modern browsers
