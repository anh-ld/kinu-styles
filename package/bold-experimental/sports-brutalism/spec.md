# Sports Brutalism

## Signature
condensed type, oversized numerals, scoreboard grids, halftone photography,
hard overlays
high contrast; `--noise: 1;`

## Suited for
sports media, streetwear, fitness, esports, events, performance campaigns

## Layout
build from scoreboard grids, oversized results, hard split screens, and
slanted overlays
keep live status, time
and primary action visible at all times

## Colors
- team colors against black and white with extreme contrast; fluorescent
  accents reserved for live
  record, win, or urgent performance states
- primary: main team color
- secondary: secondary team color
- base-100: white or tightly stepped charcoal for dark scoreboard
- base-200: cool light gray or tightly stepped charcoal
- base-300: structural gray or tightly stepped charcoal

## Imagery
direct-flash action shots, tightly cut athletes, equipment close-ups,
halftone crowds
gritty venue textures

## Density
high density through stats, fixtures, rankings, and tickers; massive numerals
and strict alignment for instant scanning; use `*-sm` size variants for
compact working density

## Motion
hard wipes, score flips, rapid counters, ticker motion, impact cuts
short camera-like shakes tied to decisive events

## Shape
hard boxes, slashes, brackets, cropped circles, thick rules
aggressive angular masks with little or no rounding
`--radius-*` set to 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
condensed heavy uppercase headlines; tabular oversized numerals for scores
plain compact sans-serif for supporting data
Suggested: Anton, Teko, Oswald, Archivo Black, Roboto Condensed

## Origins
late 2010s–present; contemporary sports media, identity systems, and digital
graphics
catalogued as Sportsbrut
collision of oversized condensed type, cropped action photography, exposed
grids, utility marks
and hard contrast
borrows the rhetoric of rawness from newer graphic and web brutalism
not the mid-century architectural movement
