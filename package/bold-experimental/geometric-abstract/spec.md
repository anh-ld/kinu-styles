# Geometric Abstract

## Signature
circles and polygons, modular geometry, bold color blocks, line systems,
repeated forms
nonrepresentational composition

## Suited for
technology branding, agencies, cultural events, portfolios, contemporary
editorial products

## Layout
repeatable geometric system on a visible or implied grid
balance large anchor forms with smaller repeated units around the content

## Colors
- limited set of bold flat colors plus a neutral field, recurring hues assigned
  to recurring geometric roles
- primary: dominant geometric hue
- secondary: recurring contrast hue
- base-100: white
- base-200: pale gray
- base-300: slightly deeper neutral

## Imagery
nonrepresentational circles, lines, polygons, modular diagrams
photography limited to tightly controlled geometric crops

## Density
medium; empty grid cells and repeated intervals keep complex arrangements
from becoming visual noise

## Motion
rotate, translate, tile, recombine forms through exact keyframes; preserve
grid relationships
and crisp edges

## Shape
defined family of circles, polygons, line weights
intersections rather than mixed unrelated decorative geometry
`--radius-*` set to 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
geometric sans-serif with disciplined alignment; echo shape proportions
through type scale
avoid expressive lettering that competes with the system
Suggested: Poppins, Jost, Montserrat, Raleway, Josefin Sans

## Origins
1910s–present; European avant-garde movements, later international;
Constructivism, De Stijl
Suprematism
later concrete art treated line, plane, shape, and color as autonomous material
interface designers inherit that vocabulary whenever circles, grids
and primary geometry organize a screen without representing physical objects
