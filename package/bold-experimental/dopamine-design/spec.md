# Dopamine Design

## Signature
saturated color clashes, bold patterns, playful shapes, cheerful icons,
oversized type
energetic layering

## Suited for
consumer apps, retail, wellness, food, events, youth-oriented brands

## Layout
energetic modular composition with oversized focal blocks and playful collisions
hold navigation and primary actions to a stable recurring track

## Colors
- saturated joyful hues in intentional warm-cool clashes; broad color fields
  and dark anchors prevent muddiness
- primary: dominant joyful warm hue
- secondary: energetic cool counterpoint
- base-100: pale lightly tinted neutral
- base-200: pale lightly tinted neutral
- base-300: pale lightly tinted neutral

## Imagery
exuberant product crops, smiling faces, tactile patterns, stickers
bold illustrations with immediate emotional warmth

## Density
medium to high visual density, moderate copy; each colorful module one short
message
and one obvious action
use `*-lg` size variants for chunky informal scale

## Motion
quick pops, spins, confetti-like success moments, bouncy transitions at key
rewards
pause motion during reading and form entry

## Shape
oversized flowers, waves, stars, squiggles, chunky pills
irregular frames with confident scale changes
`--radius-*` between 1rem and 2rem

## Typography
bold rounded or eccentric display face for compact statements; sturdy
sans-serif support
keep color clashes away from small text
Suggested: Fredoka, Paytone One, Lilita One, Modak, Titan One

## Origins
early 2020s; social-media fashion and interior trends, later adapted to
branding and UI
grew from "dopamine dressing" and "dopamine decor", especially after pandemic
restrictions
a popular label
not a scientific design theory or proven neurological effect of any palette
