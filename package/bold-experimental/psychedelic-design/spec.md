# Psychedelic Design

## Signature
warped lettering, kaleidoscopic patterns, saturated color, liquid forms,
surreal motifs
dense ornament

## Suited for
music, festivals, fashion, entertainment, experimental campaigns

## Layout
flowing radial or spiral composition with a strong center; imagery
and lettering curve outward through layered dreamlike zones

## Colors
- saturated complementary and near-neon hues in deliberate acid harmonies
  hot colors balanced with deep dark pockets for visual rest
- primary: hot magenta or orange
- secondary: acid cyan or lime
- base-100: deep purple
- base-200: near-black violet
- base-300: black

## Imagery
kaleidoscopic photography, surreal creatures, cosmic scenes, liquid marbling,
florals, eyes
hand-rendered poster ornament

## Density
high visual density, practical copy medium to low; schedules, prices
or controls on calmer solid-color fields

## Motion
morph liquid forms, rotate kaleidoscopes, breathe scale
drift layers at slow hypnotic tempos without obscuring the next action

## Shape
paisleys, spirals, melting contours, radiating petals, wavy borders
dense organic pattern with few rigid rectangles; `--radius-*` between 1rem
and 2rem

## Typography
warped or bulbous display lettering only at large sizes; plain reading face
preserve recognizable word silhouettes
Suggested: Shrikhand, Fascinate, Modak, Monoton, Yatra One

## Origins
mid-1960s–early 1970s; counterculture centers including San Francisco and London
Haight-Ashbury scene and venues such as the Fillmore produced posters with
vibrating color
compressed lettering
Art Nouveau revival lines, and optical distortion; returned through rave and
digital culture
Sources: cooperhewitt.org/2013/12/16/psychedlic-promotion,
