# Neubrutalism

## Signature
dark heavy outlines, zero-blur offset shadows, clashing flat colors,
oversized grotesque type
geometric blocks
playful exposed structure; `--depth: 1;`

## Suited for
startup sites, creator products, portfolios, cultural projects, youth brands
expressive marketing pages

## Layout
keep navigation and task flow conventional beneath an irregular modular grid
visibly bordered regions
oversize controls, occasional overlap, controlled imbalance for personality

## Colors
- black and off-white plus one to three categorical saturated accents (yellow,
  coral, blue, green
  lavender); avoid gradients or blurred atmospheric color
- primary: yellow or coral
- secondary: blue, green, or lavender
- base-100: off-white
- base-200: pale beige
- base-300: light gray

## Imagery
flat outlined illustrations, pseudo-three-dimensional objects, direct
portraits, graph-paper motifs
browser-window fragments, simple static characters with deliberately
conspicuous framing

## Density
readable medium density; clear space separating loud modules; one message,
data group
or action per panel
use `*-lg` size variants for chunky informal scale

## Motion
lift elements by increasing their hard shadow
press controls by translating them into the shadow; abrupt slides
stamps, or tilts with short direct timing

## Shape
square-cornered blocks, heavy strokes, hard shadow slabs, simple circles,
zigzags, grid cells
occasional chunky pills whose geometry stays explicit; `--radius-*` set to 0
or 0.25rem

## Typography
unusual heavy grotesque, stretched display face, or uppercase headline at
large scale
calm sans-serif or mono for body copy and functional labels
Suggested: Syne, Archivo Black, Space Grotesk, DM Sans, Space Mono

## Origins
early 2020s–present; contemporary web and product design communities
follows the raw-web revival catalogued by Pascal Deville's Brutalist Websites
from 2014
keeps conventional product usability while adding thick outlines, flat bright
color
block typography
and offset hard shadows; the label crystallized across design platforms in
the early 2020s
