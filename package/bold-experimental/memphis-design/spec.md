# Memphis Design

## Signature
squiggles, dots and terrazzo, bright geometric shapes, clashing patterns,
black accents
playful asymmetry

## Suited for
creative agencies, events, education, personal brands, energetic landing pages

## Layout
scatter bold modules asymmetrically across a clean field
balance playful imbalance with a dependable content grid and generous outer
margins

## Colors
- bright primary and pastel fields with strong black accents
  repeated combinations make the pattern language feel designed rather than random
- primary: cobalt or red
- secondary: yellow, pink, or lavender
- base-100: warm white
- base-200: pale pastel tint
- base-300: light neutral gray

## Imagery
abstract pattern, simple products, playful portrait cutouts
stylized objects integrated with dots, squiggles
and terrazzo

## Density
medium content density, lively visual density
plain regions around text counter the surrounding patterns

## Motion
bounce, rotate, slide geometric pieces independently with quick playful timing
then lock them into a balanced static arrangement

## Shape
squiggles, dots, triangles, arches, semicircles, terrazzo chips
irregular grids with bold flat outlines
`--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
chunky geometric or rounded display face for short headings
simple sans-serif that stays calm amid the pattern
Suggested: Bungee, Fredoka, Righteous, Poppins, Rubik Mono One

## Origins
founded in 1981, most influential during the 1980s; Milan, Italy
Ettore Sottsass gathered the designers
first exhibition opened September 19, 1981; laminates, clashing patterns,
bright color, asymmetry
and deliberately unstable forms challenged sober modernist good taste; the
original group was brief
but its imagery became one of the most recycled visual languages of the 1980s
