# Luminous Eco-Skeuomorphism

## Signature
strong inner and outer glow effects, luminous edge highlights, glossy
surfaces, beveled frames
recessed tracks
illuminated controls, soft bloom halos, reflective gradients, embossed panels
hardware-inspired details

## Suited for
technology dashboards, media controls, gaming interfaces, environmental products
energy applications
retro-futuristic websites, interfaces designed to feel powered or alive

## Layout
controls arranged like a compact physical console with aligned rows, grouped
modules,
and consistent spacing
hierarchy established through illumination intensity, dimensional depth,
and control size rather than large empty areas

## Colors
- dark, low-luminance foundation with one dominant luminous accent
- accent built through multiple brightness levels, from deep shadow to
  concentrated highlights
  strongest glow reserved for active or important controls
- token mapping: `primary` = luminous eco green or cyan, `secondary` =
  restrained teal or amber
  `base-100`/`base-200`/`base-300` = deep blue-gray, graphite, near-black
- `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
close-up renderings of polished materials, illuminated hardware, energy
indicators
glossy interfaces
abstract light effects; no flat illustration; imagery reinforces physical
depth and emitted light

## Density
medium to high; many compact controls organized into clearly separated
functional groups
`*-sm` size variants preserve the compact working density

## Motion
gradual light pulses, glow intensification, illuminated sweeps, indicator
flickers, slider movement
soft bloom transitions; active controls appear to power on rather than simply
change state

## Shape
rounded rectangles, pill-shaped controls, circular dials, inset tracks, thick
beveled borders
layered frames
softly chamfered mechanical forms; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
compact, highly legible sans-serif with restrained sizing
subtle illuminated edges or soft text glow on important labels
body text comparatively neutral so glowing controls dominate
Suggested: Rajdhani, Exo 2, Titillium Web, Ubuntu

## Origins
late 2010s–present; contemporary environmental branding, game UI, and product
visualization
contemporary composite label combining simulated living materials
and glowing ecological imagery with biophilic design
biophilia shaped by Erich Fromm's 1960s use, E. O. Wilson's 1984 book,
and Stephen Kellert's later translation into designed environments
Sources: biophilicdesign.umn.edu (Biophilia concepts PDF)
figma.com/resource-library/what-is-skeuomorphism
