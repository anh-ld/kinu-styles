# Mesh Gradient / Aurora UI

## Signature
multi-point gradients, luminous color clouds, soft glow, blurred transitions,
atmospheric depth
vibrant accents

## Suited for
AI products, SaaS landing pages, premium branding, onboarding, hero backgrounds

## Layout
content anchored in calm solid or lightly translucent zones while one large
aurora field
establishes the hero
section transition, or product focus

## Colors
- three to five neighboring luminous hues blended through soft multi-point fields
- quiet dark or pale zones preserved behind important text
- token mapping: `primary` = gradient's dominant luminous hue
  `secondary` = its nearest contrasting neighbor
  `base-100`/`base-200`/`base-300` = quiet near-white or deep-charcoal tones for solid surfaces
- `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
gradient treated as primary atmosphere; silhouettes, crisp product captures
or simple cutouts layered above instead of competing full-color photography

## Density
gradient-heavy regions sparse and cinematic; detailed features, forms,
and data moved into visually settled sections

## Motion
color clouds drift slowly through position and blur changes; movement stops
near reading tasks
reduced-motion preferences honored

## Shape
broad rounded frames, diffuse halos,
and occasional soft blobs echoing the color fields while functional controls
stay geometrically
clear; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
contemporary sans-serif with large, compact headlines and plain body text
paragraphs and small labels never filled with the gradient
Suggested: Sora, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Urbanist, Manrope

## Origins
late 2010s–present; contemporary digital branding and interface design
descriptive contemporary label rather than a documented movement
— extends the much older technique of continuous color transition using
modern vector
and browser tools
prominence rose alongside the saturated gradient rebrand cycle of the late 2010s
