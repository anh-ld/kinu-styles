# Chrome / Liquid Metal

## Signature
mirror reflections, molten metallic forms, liquid contours, sharp specular
highlights
dark contrast, embossed type

## Suited for
automotive, fashion, music, gaming, luxury technology, dramatic campaign sites

## Layout
cinematic stage composed around one oversized metallic object or wordmark
asymmetric editorial blocks and deep negative space for supporting content

## Colors
- black, gunmetal, silver, and blown white highlights plus one hot accent
  (electric blue
  racing red, or acid green)
- token mapping: `primary` = chosen hot accent, `secondary` = gunmetal or cool
  silver
  `base-100`/`base-200`/`base-300` = descending stack of graphite, near-black, and black
- `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
photoreal molten chrome, polished machinery, reflective products
studio imagery with deliberately visible strip lights and hard specular edges

## Density
opening experience sparse and dramatic
detailed specifications or commerce content shifts into quieter dark panels

## Motion
controlled reflections swept across surfaces; slow object rotation
metal contours morph with heavy, viscous timing

## Shape
liquid capsules, aerodynamic cuts, embossed plates
smooth metallic bulges contrasted against razor-sharp framing lines
`--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
extended geometric display faces; embossed or mirrored treatments only at
headline scale
technical copy in a restrained neutral sans-serif
Suggested: Michroma, Orbitron, Audiowide, Bruno Ace, Syncopate

## Origins
recurring since 1930s industrial styling; renewed digitally in the 2020s;
industrial design
airbrush graphics
contemporary 3D design; polished surfaces recall Streamline Moderne's 1930s
celebration
of machines and speed
later chrome-heavy airbrush and music graphics
then the early-2020s metallic branding cycle driven by accessible 3D
rendering tools
Sources: vam.ac.uk/articles/streamlined-design-speed-becomes-style
