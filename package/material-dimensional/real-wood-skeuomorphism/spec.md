# Real-Wood Skeuomorphism

## Signature
photorealistic wood grain, visible knots and pores, wooden planks, carved
controls
routed grooves, beveled edges
inset tracks, raised panels, realistic joinery, varnished surfaces, engraved
labels
material shadows

## Suited for
craft-focused brands, music interfaces, fantasy and survival games,
hospitality websites
artisan products
simulations, nostalgic applications

## Layout
interface treated as a physically constructed wooden object; planks, frames,
carved recesses
layered boards
and believable joinery organize sections; grain direction aligned with
component geometry
consistent lighting
depth, and material scale

## Colors
- palette built from natural timber finishes and variations: raw, stained,
  aged, polished, scorched
- contrasting wood species or finish intensity establishes hierarchy
  secondary materials used sparingly
- token mapping: `primary` = rich stained timber tone, `secondary` = muted
  brass or leather
  `base-100` = pale raw wood, `base-200` = mid-tone grain, `base-300` = deeper finished wood
- `--noise: 1`, `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
high-resolution realistic wood textures with visible grain, fibers, pores,
knots, scratches
cracks, saw marks
seams, and finish variation; materially authentic rather than flat brown
fills or generic
repeating patterns

## Density
moderate to dense; substantial textures, dimensional controls, decorative
framing
and material details need enough spacing to remain readable

## Motion
physical, weighty motion: buttons depressing into wood, sliders moving
through carved tracks
panels opening on hinges, controls responding with restrained spring or friction
no weightless transitions

## Shape
thick wooden slabs, carved capsules, rounded blocks, framed panels, recessed
channels
raised discs, joined planks
softened corners, irregular handcrafted edges; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem
and 1.5rem

## Typography
sturdy serif or humanist lettering that feels engraved, branded, printed, or
inset into wood
compact
highly legible labels; no ultra-thin type
dimensional text effects only when consistent with the simulated material
Suggested: Bitter, Roboto Slab, Arvo, Lora, Bree Serif

## Origins
2000s–early 2010s as a prominent UI treatment; consumer software borrowing
from furniture
audio equipment
and instrument panels; wood-grain shelves, control panels,
and desktops became visible in richly textured consumer apps of the 2000s and
early 2010s
flat design later displaced much of the simulated material
