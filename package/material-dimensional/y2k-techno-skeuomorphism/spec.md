# Y2K Techno-Skeuomorphism

## Signature
brushed silver shells, blue LCD displays, beveled transport buttons, rotary
controls
equalizer sliders
custom-shaped windows

## Suited for
music products, audio tools, games, retro utilities, hardware controllers
highly themed entertainment interfaces

## Layout
one compact custom-contoured console built from a dominant display, transport
cluster
rotary controls
equalizer bank, and docked subpanels; labels attached directly to their
hardware-like functions

## Colors
- brushed silver, graphite, gunmetal, and black around electric or icy blue LCD
  fields
- tiny red, green, or amber status lights and strong bevel highlights
- token mapping: `primary` = electric or icy LCD blue, `secondary` = muted silver
  `base-100`/`base-200`/`base-300` = stepped through graphite, gunmetal, and black
- `--noise: 1`, `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
shelf stereos, CD trays, speakers, spectrum analyzers, waveforms, machined
faceplates, screws
vents, cables
industrial consumer electronics rather than generic science fiction

## Density
dense control surfaces and terse labels; hierarchy centered on one legible
track or system display
advanced adjustments grouped into recognizable equipment bays
`*-sm` size variants preserve the compact working density

## Motion
knobs rotate, sliders snap, level meters pulse, LCD text scrolls, discs spin,
transport keys depress
indicators illuminate with believable device feedback

## Shape
irregular aerodynamic housings, beveled shells, recessed wells, machined
rims, circular dials
segmented displays
tightly fitted removable modules; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
small controls in a condensed techno sans or mono; segmented or bitmap
numerals for readouts
every abbreviation crisp at the console's dense scale
Suggested: Share Tech Mono, Orbitron, Chakra Petch, Audiowide, Michroma

## Origins
late 1990s–early 2000s source period, named retrospectively; consumer
electronics
music software, games
technology advertising; also known as Y2K Media Player UI, Chrome Futurism,
Skinnable UI
Techno Skeuomorphism
Millennium Futurism; contemporary composite label isolating Y2K interfaces
that rendered
translucent plastic
chrome hardware, cables, dials, decks, and imaginary machines
joins the millennium's cyber-futurism with the older strategy
of making digital controls resemble physical equipment
