# Cottagecore

## Signature
floral motifs, pastoral imagery, handmade textures, warm cream palette,
gentle serif type
domestic details

## Suited for
food, craft, hospitality, wellness, lifestyle, community products

## Layout
domestic journal or market table; gentle sections, recipe-like groupings
small handmade details around a clear reading path

## Colors
- primary: sage or dusty rose
- secondary: butter yellow or faded blue
- base-100: warm cream
- base-200: linen
- base-300: pale soil beige
- soft floral accents; no synthetic neon
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
pastoral landscapes, gardens, baked goods, craft, linens, cottages
candid community moments in warm natural light

## Density
medium and leisurely; useful detail grouped into labeled notes; photographs
and stories get generous room

## Motion
subtle breeze, page lifts, stitched-line draws, gentle object placement
seasonal fades with quiet handcrafted timing

## Shape
scalloped edges, floral frames, fabric labels, baskets, ribbons, rounded plaques
small imperfect domestic motifs
`--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
gentle literary serif for reading; restrained handwriting for recipes or notes
labels warm but never overly ornate
Suggested: Lora, Cormorant Infant, Alice, Gilda Display, Caveat

## Origins
Named online around 2018; global prominence in 2020
Origin: Tumblr and adjacent social-media communities
contemporary internet label for an idealized rural domestic life; precursors
in pastoral art
craft revival
and back-to-the-land culture; tag spread on Tumblr around 2018
and surged during 2020 lockdowns (baking, gardening
handwork, imagined escape); nostalgia can conceal the labor and inequality of
actual rural histories
