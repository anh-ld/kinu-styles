# Global Village Coffeehouse

## Signature
earth tones, woodcut symbols, rough borders, hand-drawn suns, natural motifs,
sincere globalism

## Suited for
coffee, food, wellness, community, travel, craft, 1990s-inspired lifestyle
brands

## Layout
handcrafted cafe menu or community board; center a sun, globe
or shared symbol among warm grouped offerings and stories

## Colors
- primary: terracotta or forest
- secondary: faded turquoise or ochre
- base-100: unbleached paper
- base-200: warm tan
- base-300: light coffee beige
- matte imperfect coverage
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
woodcut-like suns, moons, hands, spirals, plants, maps, food, craft
sincere multicultural community scenes with contextual care

## Density
medium to high detail in labeled groups; balance pattern
and storytelling with straightforward menus, schedules
or service information

## Motion
draw rough borders and symbols on, rotate suns slowly
reveal handcrafted motifs with tactile unhurried transitions

## Shape
rough borders, spirals, rays, hand-cut circles, folk symbols, vines
irregular framed panels with visible craft
`--radius-*` between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
warm humanist serif or sans-serif with hand-lettered accents; avoid
faux-ethnic display fonts
clear practical labels
Suggested: Cabin Sketch, Lora, Kalam, Alegreya Sans, Josefin Slab

## Origins
Late 1980s–mid-1990s
Origin: coffeehouse, environmental, world-music, retail, and early-internet
branding
retrospective name for warm commercial graphics combining hand drawing, earth
tones
woodcut-like marks, suns
moons, globes, spirals, and an idealized multicultural "global village"
CARI places its peak in the mid-1990s
label documented as a 2017 research-community coinage
source imagery deserves scrutiny for flattening distinct cultures into
marketable motifs
