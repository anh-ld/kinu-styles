# Frutiger Aero

## Signature
blue-green palette, glossy controls, bubbles and water, open skies, lush grass
optimistic technology

## Suited for
nostalgic campaigns, eco-tech products, playful utilities, youth experiences

## Layout
glossy utility panels over open landscape depth; clear toolbars, friendly
modules
broad sky or water scenes behind the interface

## Colors
- primary: vivid sky blue
- secondary: grass green or aqua
- base-100: bright white
- base-200: pale aqua
- base-300: light sky gray
- glassy gradients, soft shadows, optimistic high-key lighting
- texture: `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
open skies, lush grass, water, bubbles, leaves, globes, friendly technology
clean three-dimensional icons with photographic clarity

## Density
medium; compact desktop-like controls balanced with generous scenic areas
and cheerful explanatory illustrations

## Motion
float bubbles, ripple water, sweep glossy highlights, grow leaves, buoyant
window transitions
optimistic rather than futuristic

## Shape
gel buttons, glass orbs, leaf tabs, water droplets, soft bevels
rounded panels resembling friendly operating-system chrome; `--radius-*`
between 1rem and 2rem

## Typography
clear humanist sans-serif; compact interface labels; subtle softness
strong dark-on-light contrast across glossy backgrounds
Suggested: Ubuntu, Nunito Sans, Varela Round, Quicksand, M PLUS Rounded 1c

## Origins
Approximately 2005–2013; name coined retrospectively in 2017
Origin: global operating systems, stock imagery, advertising, and consumer
technology
glossy skies, grass, water, bubbles,
and friendly technology imagery common from the mid-2000s into the early 2010s
name coined by Froyo Tam and Sofi Xian in 2017
combining Adrian Frutiger's humanist type legacy with Windows Aero; a
retrospective category
not the period's own unified design system
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/frutiger-aero, cari.institute/history
