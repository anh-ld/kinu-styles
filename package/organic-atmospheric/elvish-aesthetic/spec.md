# Elvish Aesthetic

## Signature
tree-integrated architecture, leaf and star motifs, flowing filigree, slender
arches
pale metals, jewel-like light

## Suited for
fantasy games, role-playing products, publishing, film and music, immersive
events, jewelry
nature-led luxury brands

## Layout
build upward through tall arches, branching paths, layered forest clearings;
frame one character
artifact
or destination with delicate ornament while keeping the main reading path open

## Colors
- primary: forest green
- secondary: moonlit blue or pale gold
- base-100: ivory
- base-200: misted sage
- base-300: pale stone
- restrained jewel accents; sunlit woodland or cool starlit mood per view

## Imagery
ancient forests, waterfalls, tree-grown halls, hand-carved wood, fine
metalwork, bows, stars, maps
serene otherworldly figures; invent original heraldry rather than copying a
known fantasy world

## Density
low to medium; scenery and ornament get room to breathe; lore, inventories
navigation in clear layered panels

## Motion
grow vine lines, trace star paths, shimmer metal or water, drift leaves and
motes
reveal tall spaces with slow weightless motion

## Shape
elongated arches, leaf points, whiplash curves, interlaced branches,
crescents, teardrops
fine filigree instead of heavy boxes; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
elegant literary serif paired with a calligraphic or uncial display face for
short names
plain body text
never replace essential labels with invented runes
Suggested: Cormorant Garamond, Marcellus, Bellefair, Alegreya, Cinzel Decorative

## Origins
Modern fantasy lineage from the late 19th–20th centuries
Origin: European medievalism, Arts and Crafts, fantasy literature,
illustration, and film
modern fantasy category, not the art of a historical people; combines
medieval manuscript
and architectural references, Pre-Raphaelite and Arts and Crafts medievalism
Art Nouveau nature forms, and Tolkien's languages, calligraphy, maps, and
descriptions
later illustration, role-playing games, and cinema made that synthesis
globally familiar
