# Surf Crush

## Signature
surfboards and curling waves, Hawaiian and hibiscus prints, puka shells
sun-bleached photography, surfwear logos
beach bonfire ephemera

## Suited for
travel, swim and surf brands, sports, summer events, youth entertainment,
hospitality
coastal lifestyle products

## Layout
magazine-like coastal spread; panoramic wave or group photography, diagonal
board shapes
layered snapshots
sticker-style badges; logistics on clear sunlit bands

## Colors
- primary: ocean blue or aqua
- secondary: hibiscus orange or sun yellow
- base-100: white
- base-200: sunlit sand
- base-300: pale tan
- bright natural daylight; avoid muted nautical restraint and sugary coquette
  pastels
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
active surfing, swimming, volleyball, friend groups, boards, shells, beach
hair, bonfires
palm-lined shores
period surfwear; honest outdoor snapshot quality

## Density
lively visual density, moderate copy; schedules, locations, conditions, products
booking actions keep uncluttered contrast over beach imagery

## Motion
sweep sections like breaking waves, slide boards and snapshots into place,
flutter fabric patterns
brief sun glints or water ripples; not a tropical screensaver

## Shape
long surfboard ovals, curling wave arcs, hibiscus silhouettes, shells,
sunbursts, torn photo edges
repeating boardshort patterns; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
chunky surfwear sans-serif or hand-painted display lettering; relaxed
readable body face
scripts only as short sun-faded accents
Suggested: Permanent Marker, Barlow Condensed, Righteous, Nunito Sans,
Yellowtail

marketing
retrospective label for hibiscus prints, turquoise water, puka shells, board
shorts
sunlit beach branding common around the turn of the millennium
name attributed to Steves Peeps in 2018
a later archive category rather than an original industry term
