# McBling

## Signature
rhinestone sparkle, hot pink, metallic accessories, ornate scripts, leopard
print
celebrity glamour; `--depth: 1`

## Suited for
fashion, beauty, nightlife, music, entertainment, nostalgic campaigns

## Layout
celebrity tabloid, boutique window
or embellished fan page: one glamorous subject centered amid badges
product callouts, and ornamental frames

## Colors
hot pink, black, white, gold, silver, and jewel tones with rhinestone
sparkle, glossy highlights
and selective animal print
- primary: hot pink
- secondary: gold or a saturated jewel tone
- base-100/200/300: deep plum-black, near-black, black
- depth: `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
direct-flash celebrity-style portraits, handbags, jewelry, nightlife, beauty
products, faux fur
crystals
early-2000s glamour details

## Density
high visual density with short promotional copy, isolating prices, dates
and purchase actions from the surrounding embellishment

## Motion
camera flashes, rhinestone twinkles, paparazzi zooms, charm swings
glossy logo reveals with overt show-business timing

## Shape
hearts, shields, gem clusters, ornate badges, starbursts, chain-like borders
rounded glamour-photo frames
`--radius-*` between 1rem and 2rem

## Typography
mix ornate script or jeweled display type with a bold sans-serif for promotions
limiting decorative lettering to names and short statements
Suggested: Allura, Parisienne, Great Vibes, Bodoni Moda, Cinzel Decorative

## Origins
approximately 2000–2008; American celebrity, luxury, reality television,
hip-hop, fashion,
and mass retail
retrospective label for the maximal
aspirational luxury imagery that displaced much late-1990s Y2K minimal futurism
name coined by Froyo Tam in January 2017, combining a mass-market "Mc" prefix
with "bling"
— the original culture did not generally use the category name
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/mcbling, cari.institute/history
