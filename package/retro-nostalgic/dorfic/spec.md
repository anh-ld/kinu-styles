# DORFic

## Signature
sun-bleached white cityscapes, orange-red wayfinding, glass and steel,
parallel geometry
abstract tech patterns
sanitized industrial spaces

## Suited for
technology, mobility, fitness, games, workplace tools, product launches,
science communication
retro-futurist campaigns

## Layout
rigid or symmetric grid around broad white fields and one clear forward
route; orange-red bars
numbered modules
and cropped industrial diagrams make actions instantly scannable

## Colors
white dominates, orange the primary accent, red or yellow reserved for urgent
and kinetic signals
machinery grounded in gray/silver/black, cool blue added sparingly
- primary: orange
- secondary: restrained cool blue
- base-100/200/300: white, pale machine gray, silver gray

## Imagery
hard daylight, glass offices, white concrete cities, robotics, clean
factories, transit systems
techwear
protective gear, low-poly bursts; no lush nature or grimy cyberpunk clutter

## Density
medium: wide empty fields balanced with compact aligned control or data clusters
and one unmistakable accent-colored path through each view

## Motion
snap parallel panels along fixed axes, trace orange route lines, rotate
faceted structures
clean camera rushes or explosive polygon transitions without neon glitches

## Shape
parallel bands, symmetric frames, wedges, shards, faceted clusters, circles,
clipped rectangles
thin industrial lines with crisp edges; `--radius-*` 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
clean condensed or geometric sans-serif, uppercase technical labels, wide
tracking
large numerals; long copy dark
orange or red type reserved for signals
Suggested: Barlow Condensed, Rajdhani, Exo 2, IBM Plex Sans, Roboto Condensed

## Origins
mid-2000s–mid-2010s source material; term coined in 2023
retrospective online classification of technology and industrial graphics
name created on Aesthetics Wiki in April 2023, so no 2000s designer worked
under the label
— useful as a search category, not evidence of a historical movement
Sources: es.wikipedia.org/wiki/DORFic, cari.institute/aesthetics
