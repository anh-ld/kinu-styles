# Indie Sleaze

## Signature
direct-flash photography, black backgrounds, condensed type, messy cropping,
low-fi grain
nightlife ephemera
`--noise: 1`

## Suited for
music, fashion, nightlife, events, youth media, subcultural campaigns

## Layout
messy nightlife photo feed or event flyer from crooked crops and abrupt
columns, keeping dates
names
and navigation on one reliable track

## Colors
black, dirty white, flash blue, nicotine beige,
and occasional lipstick red or electric accent with harsh photographic contrast
- primary: flash blue
- secondary: lipstick red
- base-100/200/300: dark charcoal, near-black, black
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
direct-flash parties, candid bands, bathroom mirrors, street style, venue
ephemera, blurred movement
low-fi camera artifacts

## Density
high image density with compact captions
preserving blunt whitespace or solid black around lineups, credits
and calls to action

## Motion
flash cuts, jittered photo swaps, abrupt scroll reveals, timestamp flicker
short handheld-feeling transitions

## Shape
crooked photo rectangles, contact sheets, tape, scribbles, timestamp blocks,
thin rules
minimally styled form elements; `--radius-*` 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
condensed sans-serif for lineups and navigation
mixing in plain serif or handwriting accents while keeping the overall
treatment deliberately
unpolished
Suggested: Oswald, Bebas Neue, Archivo Narrow, Special Elite, Anton

## Origins
early 2000s–early 2010s; label coined retrospectively in 2018; nightlife,
fashion blogs
flash photography
music scenes, and social platforms
a retrospective label for deliberately messy nightlife imagery, direct flash
skinny type, low-resolution blogs, club flyers, and indie-electro fashion
name coined by Dalia Barillaro in October 2018, well after the scene itself
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/indie-sleaze, cari.institute/history
