# 2K1

## Signature
big red sans-serif titles, direct-flash cast photography, throwback jersey
graphics
distressed denim tones
sports insignia, DVD and music-TV chrome

## Suited for
music, entertainment, sports, youth fashion, comedy, event promotions
deliberately time-specific nostalgic campaigns

## Layout
2001 DVD cover, teen magazine spread, music-TV bumper: one oversized title
a large cast/performer crop
compact badges, tickers, supporting snapshots

## Colors
- primary: blunt red or denim blue
- secondary: khaki or brown
- base-100: dirty white
- base-200: nicotine beige
- base-300: light concrete gray
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
direct-flash friend groups, suburban parties, stadium and jersey references,
streetwear, cars
CDs, DVD packaging
broadcast captures, awkwardly confident early-digital portrait crops

## Density
medium-to-high promotional density around one loud headline, preserving
hierarchy among names
dates, ratings
tracks, and the primary action

## Motion
music-channel stings, hard camera zooms, freeze frames, scrolling broadcast
tickers
DVD-menu highlights
quick title slams, intentionally period timing

## Shape
red title bars, varsity numerals, stars, circles, ticket stubs, photo strips,
compact chrome tabs
blunt rectangular captions; `--radius-*` between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
extra-bold red grotesque or condensed lead; varsity, stencil, or slab
lettering for sports
and street references
plain early-web sans-serif supporting copy
Suggested: Anton, Archivo Black, League Spartan, Barlow Condensed, Roboto Slab

## Origins
2001–2004 (retrospective label); early-2000s sports, music, youth fashion,
games, advertising
post-Y2K transition from utopian millennium imagery toward denser commercial
styles
Sources: cari.institute/history, cari.institute/aesthetics
