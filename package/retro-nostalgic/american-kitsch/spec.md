# American Kitsch

## Signature
painted advertising scenes, smiling exaggerated characters, hand-lettered
headlines
product demonstrations
price starbursts, aged print texture; `--noise: 1`

## Suited for
food, hospitality, entertainment, heritage brands, roadside culture, packaging
humorous nostalgic campaigns

## Layout
magazine advertisement, diner placemat, billboard
or package front: one large narrative illustration
a prominent product or promise, compact benefit, price, and coupon callouts

## Colors
warm cream, cherry red, turquoise, butter yellow, mint, navy
flesh tones with vivid mid-century contrast
slightly faded ink, no atmospheric gradients
- primary: cherry red or turquoise
- secondary: butter yellow or mint
- base-100/200/300: warm cream, faded white, pale tan
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
expressive families, cooks, motorists, diners, domestic mishaps, food,
appliances, road trips
idealized suburban scenes in polished painterly realism with gentle humor

## Density
medium-to-high promotional density: short copy, badges, bursts
and labels grouped around a dominant scene while protecting the main headline
and action

## Motion
reveal the scene like a sequential print ad, illuminate diner signs, pop
price bursts
stage simple product demonstrations with brisk theatrical timing

## Shape
scalloped labels, price bursts, rounded product badges, ribbons, coupons,
speech devices
diner signs
printed frames with period-appropriate irregularity; `--radius-*` between
0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
pair brush script or an exuberant slab with a practical condensed sans-serif
limiting novelty lettering to the headline, product name, price, or short
sales phrase
Suggested: Alfa Slab One, Barlow Condensed, Libre Franklin, Lobster Two, Bitter

## Origins
late 1940s–1960s; postwar consumer culture and roadside commerce in the
United States
retrospective umbrella
not a self-named movement — draws together postwar advertising, novelty
products, roadside signs
diner graphics
tropical fantasies, household ornament, and car styling of expanding mass
consumption
exaggeration embraced as a deliberately joyful historical reference
