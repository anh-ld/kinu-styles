# Jet Age Optimism

## Signature
boomerang shapes, atomic starbursts, parabolic curves, neon signage, chrome
accents
space-age motifs

## Suited for
travel, hospitality, entertainment, retro technology, event branding,
optimistic product launches

## Layout
arrange content along sweeping parabolas and orbital paths
using diagonal launch trajectories and wide panoramic fields to create
forward-looking energy

## Colors
turquoise, coral red, sunny yellow, space navy, white,
and chrome with neon-like highlights inspired by roadside signs and aviation
- primary: turquoise
- secondary: coral red or sunny yellow
- base-100: white
- base-200: warm cream
- base-300: pale chrome gray

## Imagery
rockets, air travel, atomic symbols, futuristic buildings, starry skies,
automobiles
optimistic families rendered in polished retro illustration

## Density
medium: bold destination or product statements, small feature clusters, ample
sky-like open space

## Motion
orbit small elements, launch shapes along curves, pulse starbursts
sweep signs or vehicles across the frame with buoyant momentum

## Shape
boomerangs, atoms, parabolas, starbursts, flying-saucer ovals
sweeping fins paired with chrome-edged panels
`--radius-*` between 0.75rem and 1.5rem

## Typography
exuberant Googie-inspired display lettering for short signs
supported by a clear geometric sans-serif for schedules, features, and actions
Suggested: Monoton, Righteous, Audiowide, Fascinate, Bungee Hairline

## Origins
late 1950s–1960s; commercial aviation, corporate identity, world's fairs,
and space-age consumer culture
the Jet Age began when commercial jet travel entered regular passenger
service in the late 1950s
— Pan American's Boeing 707 service started in October 1958; airlines and
airports sold speed
and international modernity with sweeping forms, global maps, technical
sans-serifs
polished identities
