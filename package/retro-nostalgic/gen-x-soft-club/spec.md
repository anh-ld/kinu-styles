# Gen-X Soft Club

## Signature
cool muted palettes, blurred urban photography, transit-sign typography, Lomo
color casts
restrained vector overlays, sterile-organic contrast; `--noise: 1`

## Suited for
music, fashion, nightlife, culture, travel, editorial portfolios, atmospheric
lifestyle products

## Layout
restrained late-1990s album sleeve or transit campaign: one large softened
city or human image
broad negative space, crisp typographic anchors, a few small vector or
wayfinding details

## Colors
desaturated blue, seafoam or olive green, beige, tan, concrete gray, and black
binding photography and surfaces with cool color washes instead of
candy-colored Y2K chrome
- primary: desaturated blue or seafoam
- secondary: olive
- base-100/200/300: beige, cool light gray, concrete gray
- texture: `--noise: 1`

## Imagery
airports, train platforms, city skylines, concrete interiors, plants,
natural-looking portraits
blur, bloom
bokeh, vignettes, Lomo-like cross-processing, or bleach-bypass effects
without erasing the subject

## Density
low to medium: one atmospheric image and a short message dominate
while secondary navigation reads like quiet transit or album metadata

## Motion
slow crossfades, soft-focus shifts, restrained light leaks, passing-window
motion
short vector traces evoking travel and club visuals without glossy spectacle

## Shape
clean rectangular crops, thin route lines, small circles, understated
technical marks
softened photo edges
balancing sterile geometry with natural textures; `--radius-*` between
0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
neutral Helvetica-like neo-grotesque aligned like urban wayfinding
compact labels paired with spare lowercase or sentence-case headlines;
tracking measured
effects minimal
Suggested: Archivo, Inter, IBM Plex Sans, Space Grotesk, Roboto Condensed

## Origins
early 1990s–mid-2000s; lifestyle technology, wellness branding, music, and
editorial imagery
retrospective consumer-aesthetic classification rather than a name used
during its source period
— CARI credits Sloane Angel Hilton with the label, describing a polished,
translucent
nature-tinged calm as technology and wellness were sold as softer parts of
everyday life
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/gen-x-soft-club, cari.institute/history
