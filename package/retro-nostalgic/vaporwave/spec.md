# Vaporwave

## Signature
pastel neon, digital sunsets, classical busts, perspective grids, Japanese
text accents
obsolete consumer imagery
`--depth: 1`

## Suited for
music, gaming, digital art, nostalgic campaigns, experimental portfolios

## Layout
stage content above a deep perspective grid or digital horizon, floating
windows, sculptures
and product artifacts across an intentionally empty dream space

## Colors
pastel pink, lavender, cyan, peach, and deep violet with neon sunsets, faded
gradients,
and cool digital shadow
- primary: pastel pink or cyan
- secondary: lavender or peach
- base-100/200/300: deep violet, digital navy, cool near-black
- depth: `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
classical busts, obsolete consumer goods, malls, palm trees, Japanese text
accents
low-resolution skies
surreal desktop artifacts

## Density
low-to-medium copy with broad visual space, allowing nostalgia, repetition
and atmosphere to dominate before detailed information

## Motion
drift objects slowly, scroll perspective grids, degrade video with VHS texture
loop sunsets or statues with detached dreamlike pacing

## Shape
wireframe grids, floating windows, bust silhouettes, columns, palms, checker
planes
simple three-dimensional primitives; `--radius-*` 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
classical serif or retro display face paired with a clean sans-serif
using Japanese characters only when linguistically correct and contextually
meaningful
Suggested: Monoton, Syncopate, Major Mono Display, Zen Dots, Poiret One

## Origins
early 2010s; online experimental music and image communities
formed around experimental electronic releases and quickly developed a shared
visual language
of obsolete consumer media, corporate calm, classical sculpture, Japanese
text, malls,
and corrupted nostalgia; central artists include James Ferraro, Daniel Lopatin,
and Ramona Andra Xavier, whose Macintosh Plus project became emblematic;
often critique
and uneasy fascination, not simple 1980s appreciation
