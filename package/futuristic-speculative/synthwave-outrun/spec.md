# Synthwave / Outrun

## Signature
neon horizon, magenta-cyan palette, wireframe grid, chrome lettering,
sports-car silhouettes
night-drive glow
`--depth: 1`

## Suited for
games, music, automotive, nightlife, streaming, entertainment products

## Layout
a cinematic composition around a low neon horizon, deep perspective grid,
and cockpit-like foreground
keeping actions near stable screen edges

## Colors
- primary: electric magenta
- secondary: cyan
- base-100: midnight navy
- base-200: near-black blue
- base-300: black
- midnight navy and black with electric magenta, cyan, violet, and sunset orange
  chrome white for sharp highlights

## Imagery
sports cars, neon skylines, setting suns, palm silhouettes, wireframe
mountains, chrome logos
night-road photography

## Density
low to medium; one strong title, media control, or route at a time over
crowded data panels

## Motion
move the grid toward the viewer, pulse neon, scan the sun
glide vehicles or light trails with steady night-drive momentum

## Shape
triangles, horizon lines, wireframe planes, chrome capsules, sun discs
aerodynamic slashes with strong perspective; `--radius-*` between 0.25rem and
1rem

## Typography
italic techno or chrome display lettering for titles
paired with a clean condensed sans or mono for controls and track information
Suggested: Orbitron, Audiowide, Monoton, Russo One, Bruno Ace SC

## Origins
mid-2000s–present; online electronic-music scenes, especially French electro
and international independent producers
not an original 1980s genre but a later internet-era reconstruction of 1980s
film scores, games
television, and electronic music, developed in the mid-to-late 2000s; "Outrun"
— named after Sega's 1986 driving game
— became associated with the car-centered neon-sunset branch
and then the genre's wider visual shorthand
