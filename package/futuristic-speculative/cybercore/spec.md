# Cybercore

## Signature
saturated cyber blue, silver chrome, retro-anime edits, early-software
windows, flip phones
and cameras, angels
butterflies, planets; `--depth: 1`

## Suited for
music, fashion, youth communities, games, virtual creators, entertainment,
nostalgic social products

## Layout
a layered desktop or image-board collage from overlapping legacy windows,
anime crops, devices
and floating symbols, while preserving one stable readable pane for the main
task

## Colors
- primary: electric cobalt
- secondary: deep violet or pastel pink
- base-100 → base-300: harsh white, ice gray, chrome gray
- saturated cobalt and electric blue centered, with deep violet, chrome silver,
  harsh white,
  and occasional pastel pink; cold gradients, scan glow, holographic highlights

## Imagery
late-1990s anime frames, virtual idols, flip phones, digital cameras,
cursors, dialog boxes
butterflies
angel wings, hearts, moons, planets, wireframes, low-poly renders

## Density
high visual collage density but low to medium copy
essential controls separated from the nostalgic artifacts by one consistent
blue-white contrast
system

## Motion
cursor trails, window pops, CRT scans, slow orbital movement, holographic
shimmer
wistful crossfades
— rather than the aggressive alarms and corruption of cyberpunk

## Shape
thin legacy window frames, pixel cursors, chrome hearts and stars, wing
silhouettes, orbital rings
translucent discs, simple low-poly forms; `--radius-*` between 0.75rem and
1.5rem

## Typography
narrow techno or Japanese-compatible pixel face paired with a clean sans-serif
luminous or distorted lettering reserved for short decorative fragments
Suggested: DotGothic16, M PLUS 1 Code, Oxanium, Rajdhani, Zen Dots

## Origins
early-2020s revival drawing on late-1990s and early-2000s sources; Pinterest,
TikTok
and online nostalgia communities; emerged around 2020 as a curated mixture
of early digital graphics, anime
virtual-idol imagery, chrome, angels, blue light, and cyber nostalgia
the name is sometimes used for original Y2K futurism, which makes its
boundaries unstable
Sources: aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cybercore,
cari.institute/aesthetics/y2k-aesthetic
