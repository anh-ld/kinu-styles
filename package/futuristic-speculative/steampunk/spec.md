# Steampunk

## Signature
mechanical frames, exposed gears and pipes, rivets and hinges, gauges, heavy
mounted controls
layered materials
deep bevels, engraved ornament, aged patina; `--noise: 1`, `--depth: 1`

## Suited for
games, events, entertainment, museums, themed commerce, narrative experiences

## Layout
construct the whole interface as a diegetic engine, inventor's cabinet,
control panel
mechanical book
or discovered artifact; nest navigation, ledgers, gauges, and mechanisms
inside layered frames
treating each edge
hinge, fastener, and junction as part of the machine

## Colors
- primary: brass or copper
- secondary: oxblood or verdigris
- base-100 → base-300: smoky brown, dark wood, soot-black
- brass, copper, iron, dark wood, oxblood leather, yellowed parchment, smoky
  glass, cream enamel
  verdigris; soot, scratches, tarnish, rubbed edges, and uneven warm lamp light create a used
  handcrafted finish

## Imagery
controls rendered as individually made physical objects — engraved diagrams,
Victorian machinery
gears, airships
gauges, pipes, leather tools, specimen drawers, enamel labels; preserve
material grain
small imperfections
and tactile construction details

## Density
high and deliberately maximalist; engineered ornament filling panels, borders
and control clusters while protecting readable parchment fields; hierarchy
through scale
framing, lighting
and material contrast instead of broad neutral space
`*-sm` size variants preserve this compact working density

## Motion
controls with weight and causal response: depress heavy buttons, resist and
release levers
rotate linked gears
sweep pressure needles, vent brief steam, open mechanical shutters
illuminate valves in ordered sequences

## Shape
nested riveted plates, pipes, cog teeth, arched frames, valve wheels, leather
tabs, circular gauges
oversized mounted controls; depth from thick bevels, embossed trim, inset
wells, glossy highlights
hard cast shadows, stacked ornamental frames; `--radius-*` between 0.25rem
and 1rem

## Typography
Victorian display lettering paired with a sturdy book serif; engraved or
stamped small caps
plaque-mounted labels
monospaced numerals for instruments and coordinates
Suggested: Rye, Cinzel, IM Fell English SC, Pirata One, Grenze Gotisch

## Origins
literary precedents in the 1960s–1970s; term coined in 1987; Anglophone
speculative fiction
later fashion, art
games, and maker culture; writer K. W. Jeter coined the term in a 1987 letter
to Locus as a playful counterpart to cyberpunk, grouping his work with
fiction by Tim Powers
and James Blaylock; later applied backward to earlier novels and expanded
into a visual subculture
