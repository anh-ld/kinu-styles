# Utopian Scholastic

## Signature
encyclopedic collage, specimen illustrations, scientific diagrams, classical
typography
labeled objects
optimistic learning imagery

## Suited for
museums, learning products, science communication, children's media,
knowledge platforms

## Layout
organize knowledge as an inviting cabinet, atlas, or encyclopedia spread;
specimen grids
diagrams, labels
and narrative paths connected across the page

## Colors
warm paper, sky blue, leaf green, sunny yellow, mineral red,
and ink tones in optimistic educational combinations
- primary: sky blue or leaf green
- secondary: sunny yellow or mineral red
- base-100: warm paper
- base-200: cream
- base-300: light archival tan

## Imagery
scientific specimens, planets, animals, instruments, cutaway diagrams,
archival plates, maps
and labeled collections with factual specificity

## Density
high informative density through clear taxonomies, captions, legends, and
progressive layers
preserve room for curiosity and visual discovery
use `*-sm` size variants for daisyUI components to keep the compact working
density

## Motion
connect labels to specimens, orbit planets, unfold diagrams, sort collections
and reveal cross-sections in direct explanatory sequences

## Shape
specimen frames, circles, arrows, ruled labels, cabinets, orbit lines
and paper cutouts that support classification and comparison; `--radius-*`
between 0.25rem and 1rem

## Typography
classical book serif paired with a clean labeling sans-serif; consistent
caption, definition
heading
and annotation roles throughout
Suggested: Literata, Source Serif 4, Libre Baskerville, IBM Plex Mono,
Cormorant Garamond

## Origins
late 1980s–late 1990s; educational software, science museums, textbooks,
public media,
and institutional graphics
a retrospective consumer-aesthetic category for optimistic educational media
that mixed diagrams
bright geometry
science imagery, and approachable technology
term coined by CARI's research community in February 2017
not by designers during the original period
Sources: cari.institute/aesthetics/utopian-scholastic, cari.institute/history
