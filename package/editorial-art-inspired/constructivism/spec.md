# Constructivism

## Signature
dynamic diagonals, red-black palette, photomontage, bold condensed type,
geometric wedges
propaganda energy

## Suited for
culture, events, editorial projects, music, activism, historically themed
campaigns

## Layout
drive the composition along forceful diagonals; wedges, cropped circles
and stepped text propel attention toward a central statement or action

## Colors
red, black, and aged cream with occasional gray photographic tones
no soft decorative palette expansion
- primary: banner red
- secondary: ink black
- base-100: aged cream
- base-200: newsprint beige
- base-300: photographic gray

## Imagery
high-contrast photomontage, industrial machinery, workers, crowds, architecture
and paper artifacts cut into dynamic angular arrangements

## Density
high visual density and compressed slogan-like copy
secondary facts organized beneath the dominant propagandistic message

## Motion
diagonal wipes, stamped entrances, rotating wheels, rapid photomontage cuts
forceful scale changes with machine-like cadence

## Shape
wedges, beams, concentric circles, scaffolding lines, arrows
and hard photographic crops that imply momentum and collective force
`--radius-*` set to 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
bold condensed sans-serif in uppercase; rotate or step short phrases along
diagonals
supporting information compact and plainly readable
Suggested: Russo One, Oswald, Archivo Black, Roboto Condensed, Bebas Neue

## Origins
late 1910s–1930s; revolutionary Russia and the early Soviet Union
emerged from the Russian avant-garde around the 1917 Revolution
Rodchenko and Stepanova rejected autonomous art in favor of production,
communication,
and social use
dynamic diagonals, photomontage, geometric structure,
and forceful typography became common references in posters and interface
graphics
