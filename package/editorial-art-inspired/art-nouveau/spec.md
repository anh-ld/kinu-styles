# Art Nouveau

## Signature
flowing botanical lines, whiplash curves, ornamental frames, floral motifs,
elegant lettering
integrated illustration

## Suited for
beauty, hospitality, arts, weddings, premium packaging, heritage-inspired
commerce

## Layout
frame content with flowing botanical borders; tall graceful compositions
ornamental lines connect headings
imagery, and section transitions

## Colors
cream, sage, muted teal, plum, dusty rose,
and antique gold in softened botanical harmonies rather than bright digital
primaries
- primary: muted teal or plum
- secondary: sage or dusty rose
- base-100: cream
- base-200: pale parchment
- base-300: warm botanical gray

## Imagery
elegant portraiture, flowers, insects, vines, stained glass,
and integrated line illustration with a consistent hand-drawn ornamental finish

## Density
medium density; detail concentrated in frames and corner ornament
center kept calm enough for reading and action

## Motion
draw vines and whiplash curves along their paths; open floral frames
dissolve imagery gently as if revealed through layered print

## Shape
whiplash curves, tendrils, petals, elongated arches, oval frames
intertwined organic borders with few straight mechanical edges; `--radius-*`
between 0.75rem
and 1.5rem

## Typography
reserve decorative period lettering for names and short headings
elegant readable serif with generous leading for all substantive copy
Suggested: Poiret One, Elsie, Cormorant Infant, Marcellus, Berkshire Swash

## Origins
early 1890s–World War I; international movement with major centers in
Belgium, France, Austria
Britain, Spain
and the United States; emerged in the 1890s as an attempt at a modern unified
art spanning
architecture, interiors
posters, type, and objects; flowing plant-like lines, asymmetric composition
and integrated ornament became common signals; World War I largely ended its
first period
of prominence
