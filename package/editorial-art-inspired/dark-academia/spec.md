# Dark Academia

## Signature
old-style serif type, parchment tones, dark wood, archival imagery,
marginalia, scholarly ornament

## Suited for
reading apps, museums, education platforms, literary brands, knowledge tools

## Layout
organize content like a library desk or annotated volume; reading columns,
marginal notes
catalog panels
and layered archival references

## Colors
parchment, dark walnut, forest, burgundy, tarnished brass,
and ink black with subdued contrast that still supports long reading
- primary: forest or burgundy
- secondary: tarnished brass
- base-100, base-200, base-300: dark walnut, ink brown, near-black
- content: parchment-toned base content
- texture: `--noise: 1`
- depth: `--depth: 1`

## Imagery
books, manuscripts, maps, museum objects, portraits, libraries
and botanical or scientific plates with archival captions

## Density
medium to high reading density; clear sections, footnotes, indexes
and margins rather than simplifying scholarly material

## Motion
page turns, drawer or index reveals, slow dust and light effects
subtle underlining tied to exploration

## Shape
bookplates, ruled margins, catalog tabs, wax-seal circles, engraved frames
and rectangular paper layers with slight age; `--radius-*` between 0.25rem
and 1rem

## Typography
long text in an old-style serif; small caps and italics for scholarly hierarchy
display faces reserved for chapter-like openings
Suggested: EB Garamond, Cormorant Garamond, Libre Baskerville, Crimson Pro,
IM Fell English

## Origins
online formation in the 2010s; mass visibility around 2020; Tumblr
and other transnational social platforms
an internet aesthetic rather than a historic academic style
— scholarship traces its literary imagination to Donna Tartt's 1992 novel The
Secret History
and its community formation to 2010s Tumblr fandom
pandemic-era social media broadened the look of old universities, classical
art, uniforms
candlelight, and melancholy study
