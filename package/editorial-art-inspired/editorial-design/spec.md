# Editorial Design

## Signature
display serif headlines, multi-column grid, art-directed photography, pull
quotes, captions
varied pacing

## Suited for
publishing, fashion, portfolios, cultural institutions, premium e-commerce

## Layout
alternate multi-column reading pages and full-bleed visual spreads
pull quotes and narrow caption rails create magazine-like pacing across the
experience

## Colors
paper and ink neutrals first, then one issue-like seasonal accent drawn from
the lead photography
or subject matter
- primary: the issue's seasonal accent
- secondary: quieter ink or photo-derived companion
- base-100: clean paper
- base-200: warm off-white
- base-300: pale newsprint

## Imagery
art-directed photography with deliberate crops, captions, credits, sequencing,
and occasional full-viewport impact

## Density
vary rhythm on purpose: dense reading passages alternate with sparse
image-led pauses instead
of giving every section equal weight

## Motion
scroll-linked crop reveals, page-like masks, caption entrances
restrained crossfades preserving the feeling of turning through a publication

## Shape
column boundaries, hairline rules, image frames, folio marks
inset quotations over generic rounded cards
`--radius-*` set to 0 or 0.25rem

## Typography
pair a characterful display serif with a disciplined sans-serif
clear headline-deck-body-caption ladder
tune line length and leading for sustained reading
Suggested: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Newsreader, DM Serif
Display, Libre Baskerville

## Origins
print lineage from early periodicals; digital adaptation since the 1990s;
newspaper, magazine
and book publishing across Europe and the Americas
a professional field rather than one visual movement — columns
headlines, captions, and image hierarchy transferred
to the web as publications preserved editorial pacing on variable screens
modern digital versions often combine that lineage with Swiss grids and
art-direction traditions
